SoccerDisc — Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game SoccerDisc (the “App”), published on Google Play by Bruno Bagalà. (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use it.

In short: the App has no user accounts and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Your game progress stays on your device. Some data is processed by Google services that the App uses for advertising, purchases and anonymous usage statistics, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device
Game data: careers, tournaments, settings, results and records are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
Images and files you import in the EDIT section (for example team logos or trophy images, or .sdpack files) are copied into the App's private storage on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.
Purchase status: the list of content packs you own is cached on your device so the App also works offline.
2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, device model and operating system, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time from the App's settings. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)
Optional content packs are sold through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google: we never see or store your card or payment details. The App only receives from Google Play the information needed to unlock what you bought (the product, the purchase state and a purchase token and signature used to verify it). See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)
To understand how the App is used and improve it, we use Google Analytics for Firebase. It records events such as which screens are opened and for how long, the App version, device model, operating system, approximate country, and an app-instance identifier. These statistics are used in aggregate and are not used to identify you. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. What we do not do
We do not sell your personal information.
We do not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.
We do not run our own servers that collect data from the App.
6. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)
Personalised advertising and analytics are based on your consent where required by law; non-personalised advertising, purchase processing and keeping the App secure are based on our legitimate interests and on the performance of the contract when you buy a content pack.

7. Data retention
Data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies linked above.

8. Children
The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold personal data on our own systems, most requests can be handled directly through your device settings or the Google services linked above. For anything else, contact us at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the App or on its Google Play page.

11. Contact
